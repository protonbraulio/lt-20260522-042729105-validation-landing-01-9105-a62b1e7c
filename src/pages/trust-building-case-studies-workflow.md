## Detailed explanation

The trust-building case studies workflow involves several key steps and stakeholders, starting with the marketing lead who initiates the process.

First, the marketing lead defines the case study's purpose, target audience, and key messages. They then assign a writer to draft the case study and coordinate with relevant team members to gather data and insights.

The writer conducts interviews, gathers documents, and crafts a compelling narrative that highlights the business's strengths, unique selling points, and successful outcomes. They also ensure the case study adheres to the company's brand guidelines and tone of voice.

Once the draft is complete, the marketing lead reviews it, providing feedback and ensuring it aligns with the initial objectives. They may also involve other stakeholders, such as the subject matter expert or the client, in the review process.

After incorporating feedback, the marketing lead approves the final case study and coordinates its publication on the company's website or other marketing channels. They also track its performance, using metrics such as downloads, shares, and engagement, to assess its impact on trust-building and lead generation.

## Common mistakes

To avoid common pitfalls, marketing leads should steer clear of these mistakes when implementing the trust-building case studies workflow in Toronto:

**Focusing on features instead of benefits**: Case studies should emphasize the value and outcomes achieved, rather than just listing product or service features.

**Including too much detail or irrelevant information**: Keep case studies concise and focused on the most important aspects of the success story.

**Neglecting to gather data and insights from multiple sources**: To ensure accuracy and completeness, involve relevant team members and clients in the data gathering process.

**Rushing the review and approval process**: Take the time to gather and incorporate feedback from stakeholders to ensure the final case study is polished and effective.
